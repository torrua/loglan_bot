About Loglan

Invented in 1955 by Dr. James Cooke Brown, Loglan was conceived as a speakable language based on first-order predicate logic. Its grammar is regular and designed to parse without syntactic ambiguity.

For learning materials and historical reference, visit our How to Read Guide or browse the Lognet Columns Archive.
