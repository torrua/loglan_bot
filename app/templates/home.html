About Loglan

Invented in 1955 by Dr. James Cooke Brown, Loglan was conceived as a speakable language based on first-order predicate logic. Its grammar is completely regular and parses without ambiguity.

For learning materials and historical reference, visit our How to Read Guide or browse the Lognet Columns Archive.
